# Brand authenticity review: jessekarkoukly.com homepage

## 1. Verdict

The top and bottom feel like Jesse. The sunlit arch headshot makes direct eye contact, and the page opens with "What brings you here?" and closes with "Every door, walked through together." The middle doesn't sound like him: the broker deck and About praise him in stock phrases ("I am transparent, strategic and understanding", "invested in your success") instead of using his own words. The biggest problem is that the page never tells his real 2021 story, so the About promise has nothing behind it and nobody who has met him would recognize the calm, humble connector.

## 2. Findings, ranked by impact

1. **His origin story is missing (Must).** About gives a generic reason that includes a banned phrase ("one of the biggest financial decisions of their adult lives"). The About page tells a second story and the landing page a third, which is the true one. Use the 2021 story everywhere (R1).
2. **He praises himself where clients should do it (Must).** "Humble but polished" means clients make the claims, and they already have. Calm: "What impressed me most was how calm and responsive he remained throughout." (Ephy Bookbinder). Honest: "giving me honest advice that you wouldn't expect from most brokers" (David Stern). Guzzi's "in our corner" is his guiding idea, in her words (R2, R3, R9).
3. **Wrong licence class (Must).** "As a mortgage broker, I have access to over 50 lenders." He is a Mortgage Agent Level 1 (R4). FSRA's advertising guidance aims to keep consumers from being "misled by deceptive or confusing information". Worth a quick check with Sherwood.
4. **Lender logos (Must).** See V1.
5. **The things that make him him are missing (Should).** The mantra, the connector habit, The Annex and being a dad are all absent. The tech decade has no "so what", and his vision supplies it (R5, R6, R8).
6. **Access is never shown (Should).** The page never says "call or text" (R7). Make no hour or same-day promises; CLAUDE.md dropped "Response within 24 hours" for that reason. Let reviews prove speed: Konjek says "fast and friendly".
7. **Smaller fixes (Should).**
   - "the lenders we apply to" becomes "I pull your credit once, only with your OK, and use it for every lender I send your file to."
   - "You always get the better rate… Either way, you end up with the best available number." becomes his email wording: "If rates drop before closing, you take the better one. If they go up, you keep your hold."
   - The reversal "Starting early is not about rushing a decision…" becomes "Starting early gives you time to compare options without rushing."

### Off-brand visual notes

- **V1 (Must).** Cut the 15 lender logos. The brand book says "Don't show lender names or logos". The logos are also recoloured with a CSS filter, "Partners" overstates the relationship, and the logos grow on hover. If a line is needed: "I compare options from 50+ lenders, big banks included."
- **V2 (Should).** Five dark bands is too heavy for a California, sun-drenched brand whose ratio is linen 60, clay 25, espresso 12, sky 3. Keep one dark block mid-page plus the footer. Fold "Mortgage questions" into the clay "Every door" band. Keep the sky deep reviews band (his call).
- **V3 (Should).** There are four different-looking Jesses on the page. Swap the darker, bearded headshot on the LinkedIn blog card for a Toronto homes image. Put the brand headshot in a clay ring by the closing note.
- **V4 (Should).** The broker deck's cartoon hand is playful but not calm. A light "Your bank / Me" comparison would read more like him.
- **V5 (Could).** The About button lifts and glows, and the FAQ icons scale. The brand book says "Colour change only… No movement, no growing shadows."
- **V6 (Could).** The Google-style review cards (Arial, rainbow initial circles) are off-palette. Use the landing page quote cards and keep the Google G and the count.
- **V7 (Could).** The footer P4 is flattened to white by a CSS filter. Use the sand-to-linen version he chose for dark backgrounds.

## 3. Keep exactly as is

- The arch headshot on linen.
- "Confident mortgage decisions", "What brings you here?" and "Every door, walked through together." with the P4.
- "You should understand exactly what you're signing, and I make sure you do." (with the contraction).
- "My job is to get you from the starting point to keys in hand."
- "Often yes, sometimes no."
- The "Hi Jesse, I'm ___" story form, the family walk photo, and the sparkler.

## 4. Recommendations

**Order (brand angle).** Hero, What brings you here?, reviews, About with the 2021 story, broker vs bank, How it works, FAQ, blog, help list. Then a clay close: a short note from Jesse beside his clay-ring headshot, then "Every door, walked through together." The story goes before the argument because his brand book says "Clients choose who they like, who feels genuine."

### Hero options

Under the buttons, show stars and "19 five-star reviews on Google" from the live count.

- **A.** "Confident mortgage decisions start here." / "I'm Jesse, a mortgage agent in Toronto. I work with first-time buyers and self-employed borrowers across Ontario." *Keeps both approved lines and adds his name.*
- **B (my pick).** "You should understand exactly what you're signing." / "I'm Jesse, a Toronto mortgage agent with Sherwood Mortgage Group. I help first-time buyers and self-employed borrowers make confident mortgage decisions." *His brand statement, earned by the story below it, with the descriptor word for word. About's heading becomes "I learned this the expensive way."*
- **C.** "Confident mortgage decisions, with a real person behind them." / "I'm Jesse, a Toronto mortgage agent. You deal with me from the first call to closing day, and I pick up the phone." *The Human pillar beside the eye-contact photo.*

### Guard rails

- He was licensed in late 2025. Don't use "years of experience", "hundreds of families", "expert" or "top" for him.
- Use the live review count (19), never "20+". Quote reviews word for word, whole sentences only.
- Family details are limited to "I'm a dad", The Annex and the public "my wife and I". jesse-family.jpg shows readable house numbers, so check before reusing it.
- Keep to one playful line and one playful motion. No exclamation marks.
